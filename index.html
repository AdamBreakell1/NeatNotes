FAQ

Is Neat Notes free?

Yes. Free accounts can create notes and choose one OCR revision deck to try.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks the full OCR library, unlimited notes, exports, version history, collaboration and full Quick Practice.

Can teachers create classes?

Yes. Teacher mode supports class creation, join codes and class insight tools.

Is it only for OCR A-Level Computer Science?

The current product focus is OCR A-Level Computer Science. The structure can expand to other courses as the product grows.

Can I cancel anytime?

Paid plans are managed securely through Stripe, including billing changes and cancellation.

How is student data handled?

Student and class data is used to provide the workspace, revision tracking and teacher insight. Schools can request export or deletion support.
