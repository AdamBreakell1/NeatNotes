# Durable subscription confirmation

New monthly Student Pro Checkout sessions require the current terms, adult permission, a configured legal operator, postal address and support email, and an explicit HTTPS production origin before contacting Stripe. The app stores the accepted policy version, time, monthly price, operator details and complete self-contained Terms and Cancellation and Billing HTML against the unique Stripe Checkout session. The original snapshot cannot be replaced by another account or a later policy version. A SHA-256 snapshot reference accompanies the Checkout metadata.

After a signed Stripe completion event confirms paid or no-payment-required status and an active or trialling recognised subscription, one database transaction applies the entitlement, records completion and creates the transactional confirmation outbox entry. Delayed unpaid Checkout completion grants no access or confirmation; its signed `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded` event can complete the purchase. A unique session entry prevents duplicate Stripe events from repeating the entitlement or confirmation. A database failure rolls back the transaction so Stripe can safely retry.

The branded HTML and plain-text email includes the recurring price, actual Checkout amount when supplied, paid-period end, renewal and cancellation instructions, the full 14-day refund promise, support details and operator identity. Customers can save three attachments: a plain-text confirmation and the original accepted Terms and Cancellation and Billing HTML. These are stored copies, rather than links to a page that may change. Account export includes the original contract and confirmation delivery status.

Email delivery runs after the entitlement transaction and uses a 12-second bounded transport. A provider failure leaves the webhook successful and paid access intact, stores only an allowed error code, and schedules the immutable message for retry ten minutes later. Cloudflare's Durable Object alarm and the Node process timer retry independently of optional usage analytics or contact delivery. A persistent two-minute lease prevents simultaneous send claims and permits recovery after a process stops. The same Message-ID is reused. SMTP delivery is at least once: if a provider accepts a message but its acknowledgement is lost, a retry may produce another copy. This does not repeat billing writes or charges.

Old Checkout sessions with no recorded snapshot receive an explicitly identified historical confirmation with the original amount and billing interval left to Stripe. They receive no invented acceptance timestamp, terms version, monthly-price promise or annual-commitment assertion. The app does not silently substitute today's policy as their historical agreement.

Contract snapshots, message recipients, confirmation contents and delivery status are retained for at most seven years while the account exists. Daily maintenance removes older rows. Account deletion cascades both tables immediately; another customer's documents remain isolated. These are transactional subscription records, separate from optional analytics. Access to them uses the existing account authorisation and Cloudflare database controls.

Validation on 10 October 2026 used synthetic accounts, injected Stripe and SMTP transports, and an in-memory SQLite database. Seven service tests passed for immutable documents, MIME attachments, unique identities, ten-minute retry/restart, concurrent and abandoned leases, safe provider errors, truthful legacy handling, retention and erasure. Six HTTP integration tests passed for signed paid completion, duplicate events, exported documents, cross-account isolation, account deletion, email-failure independence, restart recovery preserving the original attachments, missing public configuration, invalid signatures, delayed payment success, transactional rollback and legacy completion. The actual local Wrangler/workerd SQLite Durable Object compatibility fixture also passed contract/outbox persistence across requests, unique insertion, atomic rollback, persisted ten-minute retry, leased delivery with three attachments and the same Message-ID, and account-deletion cascade. No real payment or subscription-confirmation email was sent by these tests.

The Cloudflare production deployment must include this code before these guarantees apply to new purchases. SMTP sender/domain verification and Stripe's live configuration are separate provider checks owned by the operator; tests do not assert a real provider accepted mail or took a payment.
